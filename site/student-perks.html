Azure for Students

Microsoft currently lists $100 credit for eligible full-time university students, usable within 12 months, plus specified free service amounts. Your signed-in account currently shows the subscription as Disabled. Microsoft says the cause can be used-up credit, expired credit, or accidental cancellation. Check the sponsorship balance and reason first. Reactivation after accidental cancellation may go through support; an expired or depleted credit may require a paid upgrade to continue.
