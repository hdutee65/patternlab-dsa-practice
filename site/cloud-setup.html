Your progress, wherever you practice.

Cloud accounts are connected. Create an account, confirm your email, then sign in on each device to sync your drafts, attempts, hints, and study notes.

Start syncing

  1. Return to PatternLab home and choose Sign in / Create account.
  2. Create an account with your email and a password, then confirm the email from Supabase.
  3. Come back to PatternLab and sign in. Your local study trail will be backed up to your account.
  4. Sign in on another device to restore your progress there. Keep using the site offline; local progress syncs when you are signed in and back online.

You can continue without an account. Use the Progress page to export a JSON backup if you want to carry local progress manually.

Install PatternLab

Use the Install button in the top bar when your browser offers app installation. On iPhone or iPad, open the browser Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.

Security and privacy

The site uses only a browser-safe publishable key. Row-level security limits each saved progress record to its signed-in owner. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; PatternLab never stores them. Never put a secret or service-role key in this website.

Account sync is opt-in. Your unsynced progress remains in your browser, and code submissions run locally in browser workers.
